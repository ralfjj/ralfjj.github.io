About Ralf Jeeser

I started on film sets in 2019 as a builder/carpenter and set dresser.

Eight years and more than 20 big productions later I've run construction crews, supervised set dress teams and assisted alongside some of the best production designers and art directors of Scandinavia.

I aspire to become one of the well‑known names of on‑set art departmentry.
