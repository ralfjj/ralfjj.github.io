The Swedish Torpedo

Behind the scenes, 10 photosPhotos and loops: Ralf Jeeser

Directed by Frida Kempff. Production designer Elle Furudahl (IMDb). Estonian, Swedish, Finnish and Belgian co-production; parts shot on Saaremaa, Estonia.

01Sõrve lighthouse

on location

A white lighthouse on open grass with a gravel track beside it. Crew gather at the door, a film truck is parked with its ramp down.

02Boats on the shore

in the film

Two wooden rowing boats pulled up at the water's edge, a man stepping out with a suitcase. A flag is rigged on the bow of the front boat.

03Hotel rooms

in the film

A dining room with a long wooden bar, bottles in a lit cabinet, laid tables and lamps. A hall with a red carpet and a framed painting on the wall. A crowded lobby seen from above, with period uniforms and hats.

04Map table and dark room

in the film

Naval officers around a table with a large map, books on shelves behind them. A dark panelled room with a long table and a group of people around it.
