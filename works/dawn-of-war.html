Dawn of War

Behind the scenes, 15 photosPhotos and loops: Ralf Jeeser

Directed by Margus Paju. Production designer Jaagup Roomet, art director Kamilla Kase (IMDb). Amrion. 1939, Estonia.

01Plans and drawings

Production design drawings by Jaagup Roomet: a site plan of tennis courts, an elevation of a long counter, and a cabinet with rows of small drawers.

02Offices and rooms

A wood-panelled room with a framed portrait on the wall. An officer's office with a map of Europe, a flag on the desk, a lamp and telephones. A plain white room with a panelled door.

03Telephone exchange and tennis court

Switchboard operators at a wall of sockets and cables. A red clay tennis court in front of a white fence, with a brick tower behind.

04Outdoors and period dressing

A motorcycle at the roadside, a black car on a gravel road, a barn by the water with a motorcycle. A canvas tent camp with sandbags and soldiers. Cyclists and crowds in front of a red brick building. A crowded dressed ballroom with chandeliers.
