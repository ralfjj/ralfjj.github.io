The Twin

Behind the scenes, 16 photosPhotos and loops: Ralf Jeeser

Directed by Taneli Mustonen. Production designer Tiiu-Ann Pello (IMDb). Don Films. Photos by Heikki Leis.

01The house

A timber-framed villa with steep roofs in a pine wood, once in snow beside a pond and once on bare ground with a van parked in front.

02The timber frame in the clearing

A tall frame of raw timber poles being raised by a crowd in a clearing, then up close with the cast on the ground around it. Rough wooden rails around the field.

03The barn

A huge dark timber hall with beams, a long open door at the end and light coming through the slats. A barn interior with plank walls.

04Rooms in the house

A child's room with a round window, patterned wallpaper, a toy railway on the rug. An attic full of furniture, trunks and a round table. A dark hallway with a round window at the end. A living room with a low table, books and tea.
