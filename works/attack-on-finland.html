Attack on Finland

Behind the scenes, 13 photosPhotos and loops: Ralf Jeeser

Directed by Aku Louhimies. Production designer Kaisa Mäkinen (IMDb). Based on Ilkka Remes' novel Itsenäisyyspäivä. Shot in Finland and Estonia [CHECK: which scenes were shot in Estonia].

01Design boards

Production design boards by Kristina Lõuk. A government jet: cockpit with blue screens, a cabin of white seats, and a fuselage section standing on a frame in a dark hall. A long concrete corridor drawn with light lines. A hangar interior with figures standing in it.

02Control rooms

Wall of monitors with maps and radar, desks with headsets and screens, uniformed staff standing around. A dark room with one woman at a laptop.

03Halls and reception

A hall with white columns and flower arrangements, a reception with a waiter and guests, a formal couple in a doorway. A bare industrial hall with tall windows and bundled stock on the floor. A director's chair stands in a large empty room behind an actor.

04Winter shooting

The director at the camera in snow, with a black jeep parked behind and crew in masks.
