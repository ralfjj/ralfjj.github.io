Mädät omenat

Behind the scenes, 28 photosPhotos and loops: Ralf Jeeser

Directed by Marja Pyykkö and Pamela Tola. Production designer Kristina Lõuk (IMDb). Series by Fire Monkey, set in the 1970s. Photos by still photographer Heikki Leis.

01Opening scenes

A street protest with hand-painted signs. A woman behind the bars of a cell with a guard and an official in front. A harbour quay with a small ship and two people walking.

02The building and its corridors

A two-storey manor with red-painted walls and a stepped entrance. Long institutional corridors with high windows, a bench and tiled floors. A hallway with green wall panels and people standing at doors.

03Surgery, ward and offices

A surgery with a lamp and a nurse in a white uniform, a stool and a hospital trolley, a tray of surgical tools. A ward bedroom with floral wallpaper and two wall lamps. A doctor's desk with books and a typewriter, and an office with a film projector on a shelf.

04Doctors, nurses and small sets

A line of doctors in white coats with folders. Nurses in white caps and uniforms. A dress rack hung in a window. Two board views of small sets: a wooden shed with a sink, pots and shelves of jars, and a store shelf with enamel bowls.

05Outdoors: coast, forest, garden

A stony beach and a cliff on the coast, a woman on the shore, a laid table with a tea set and apples facing the sea. A forest path. A blue wooden house with a lawn, with a small fire on the grass. A crate of apples on the lawn with washing on a line.
