Azrael

Behind the scenes, 17 photosPhotos and loops: Ralf Jeeser

NB: This is a spoiler and might ruin some of the movie magic for you.

Directed by E.L. Katz. Production designer Carlos Laszlo, art director Kaia Tungal (IMDb). Shot in Harju County, Estonia.

01Forest location

autumn

Pine forest with sparse undergrowth, shot on open ground and between trees. The crew works in the forest with camera and small lights; the set is the forest itself.

02Objects hung in the trees

Small objects hang from branches across the clearing, and a lamp on a pole stands among the crowd. Boulders and low brush are left as they are.

03Shooting in the forest

Camera crew set up between the trees, actors and crew standing close in cold weather. Boulders and stumps in the foreground.

04The wooden church

in the film

Film stills of a timber church interior: pews, an altar with a cross, a round window, candles and oil lamps on every surface.

05Forest in the film

Stills of the same forest: a stone ruin among fallen trees, a woman kneeling in moss, a group standing around a chair in a clearing.
