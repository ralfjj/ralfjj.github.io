Kalev

Behind the scenes, 18 photosPhotos and loops: Ralf Jeeser

Directed by Ove Musting. Production designer Tiiu-Ann Pello, art director Anna-Liisa Liiver (IMDb). Allfilm, Ugri Film. 1991, Estonia.

01Flats, 1991

Soviet-era apartments: a living room with a television, a table of food and a sideboard. A red-lit corner with carnations and a rotary phone. A bedroom with a bookshelf, radio, wall photos and a patterned rug. A sofa with cushions and a low table laid with tea cups. A room with a TV seen through a doorway.

02Offices

Large offices with dark wood shelving, desks stacked with paper, a wall map, and a waiting area with maroon armchairs and a low table.

03Sports halls

Wooden-floor basketball halls with stands full of spectators, banners and a scoreboard end. Players on court in blue and white.

04Locker room, meeting room, bus

A tiled locker room with shower pipes on the wall. A coach talking to the team on benches. A long table in a panelled meeting room under a ceiling light. A yellow bus parked in front of a grey school building.
