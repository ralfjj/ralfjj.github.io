Burial

Behind the scenes, 15 photosPhotos and loops: Ralf Jeeser

NB: This is a spoiler and might ruin some of the movie magic for you.

Directed by Ben Parker. Production designer Jaagup Roomet, art director Kamilla Kase (IMDb). Bleiburg Productions. May 1945, in a forest.

01Drawings

Production design drawings by Jaagup Roomet: a pencil sketch of a tavern bar wall with shelves and a cross, a sketch of a tavern room with long tables and hanging lamps, a coloured drawing of a bedroom with a wardrobe, and a technical elevation of a long timber building.

02The tavern

A wooden tavern with a painted sign over the porch door, in a green clapboard house. Inside: a counter with two stools, shelves of jars, a barrel, a cross on the wall and an oil lamp hanging above. Soldiers at a table under a hanging lamp, with a stag skull on the wall in the second room.

03The bedroom

A small room with a bed under a floral cover, a bookcase full of books, a radiator and a chest of drawers.

04Cellar and barn

A cellar with a stone wall and a stair, planks and an ammunition box on the floor, lit through gaps. A stair and doorway with light coming through the boards.
