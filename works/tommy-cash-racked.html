01Church and chapel

A stone church interior with white-robed figures, a red carpet and tall windows. A figure in a ruff collar between two figures in white mitres. Nuns' headdresses at a balcony rail in a beam of window light.

A stone courtyard with a crowd in long coloured costumes, a dais with a throne and a canopy. Figures swinging on posts in the yard. Knights in full armour fighting in the open. A ring of swords pointing in toward a face. A group of women in layered period dresses.
