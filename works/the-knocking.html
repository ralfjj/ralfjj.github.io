The Knocking

Behind the scenes, 7 photosPhotos and loops: Ralf Jeeser

Directed by Max Seeck and Joonas Pajunen. Production designer Kristina Lõuk (IMDb). Finnish folk horror, a Don Films production.

01Design boards: house and farm

Production design boards by Kristina Lõuk, made from frames of the film. A dark wooden house interior with shelves, doors and a window wall. A dark timber barn with plank walls, stacked wood and a vehicle inside.

02Design boards: roadside and forest

A board with a car park and roadside, a small kitchen and a bookshelf room. A board with people in a forest at a fallen log, and a ring of cut wood slices laid on the leaves.

03In the film

The wood slices in close-up, laid on the forest floor. An empty car park at the forest edge. A red car at the roadside among trees.
