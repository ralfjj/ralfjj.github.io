Sisu: Road to Revenge

Behind the scenes, 48 photosPhotos and loops: Ralf Jeeser

Shot almost entirely in Estonia: Ida-Viru, Harju, Rapla and Lääne counties. 44 shooting days, a 105-person local crew. Estonia stands in for post-war Finland and Karelia.

Set diary, June to October 2024. Every photo and loop below was taken on the job.

01Prep in the studio

June

A studio hall with a wood-panelled interior set going up and a monitor screen built into the wall. The set is standing before anyone is in front of a camera.

02Timber and the truck

late July

Reclaimed beams come in on pallets. The blue truck gets its load: old squared timber hung off the cab side on an aluminium frame, for the log rig. In the hall, plywood floor panels and steel frames lie out flat, and black truss frames stand ready.

03Loading

August–September

The crane in the hall lifts a platform of boards onto the truck bed. The truck in the film, then both log trucks loaded and parked at base.

04Brick prison gate

August

A barred door in a brick wall, then the same door framed and aged, then the yard behind it with the stone wall.

05Forest road and field

August–September

A wrecked aircraft hull lies on a gravel road between pines. Later a log barricade across a track and steel hedgehogs in long grass. A dummy body in the grass. A wrecked car on a concrete road, then up close, then the log truck driving straight through it, on the monitor.

06Vehicles and timber on location

September

Timber laid out on sand near the coast.

07Back in the studio: crates and blue screen

late Sept

Timber stack and ammunition crates in the hall. A crate frame built on a platform while loose beams lie in front. Stacked crates, and one crate with bullet hits. A blue-screen stage with a stunt rig on a crane.

08The train

late Sept–Oct

First a test shoot: the wagon set in the hall with a screen standing behind its windows, checked on the monitors. Then the long train platform on tyres. The dining car after the fight, the wagons opening one into the next with blue screen at the end, and the same blue screen through a wagon door on the monitor. Last, the wagons as they read in the film.

09Tank and water work

October

Then an indoor water tank: safety line, dive crew, platform and gantry. A log is rigged underwater.

10Wrap

October

Props store, barrels, milk cans, baskets, shelving. Last, reclaimed boards in a farm building. What comes out of the shoot goes back on the shelf or the pile.
