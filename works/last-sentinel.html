Last Sentinel

Behind the scenes, 14 photosPhotos and loops: Ralf Jeeser

Directed by Tanel Toom. Production designer Kaia Tungal, art director Jaak Sova (IMDb). Allfilm, Altitude Film Entertainment. Estonia, Germany, United Kingdom.

01Design boards

Production design boards by Kaia Tungal. The sea fort seen from below: a square steel house on four legs, with a lookout on top. A rowing boat on water full of floating wreckage and planks. A control room with dials, clocks and a radio desk.

02The fort and the sea

The fort in a heavy sea, from outside. A soldier at the gun deck with a rail and plank walls, and a gun position with a helmeted gunner. An old ship at anchor next to the fort.

03Inside the fort

Grey steel rooms: a mess with long white tables and folding chairs, a command room with wall clocks, dials and a radio desk, a work room with shelves and tools in warm light.

04Wreckage on the water

A rowing boat among floating debris, planks and crates, shot from above.

05On a ship deck

Camera, boom and crew on a grey steel deck, with rigging and sky behind.
